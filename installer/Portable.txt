GameScreen Portable

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder you can write to.
2. Keep portable.flag next to GameScreen.exe.
3. Open GameScreen.exe and approve the Windows administrator prompt.

Your settings are stored under Data\<computer name>\settings.json in this folder.
On a different PC, select its gaming display and add any local game executables.
To move GameScreen Portable, quit it from the tray and move the entire folder.
